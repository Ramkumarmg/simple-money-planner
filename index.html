Goal by goal

Each goal gets its own plan: near goals stay safe, far goals can grow in shares. Tap "See plan" for the full split and example funds.

Which goal comes first? 1) Emergency fund (6 months of expenses), 2) term and health insurance, 3) retirement, 4) children's education, 5) house, 6) car, travel and other wants. If money is short, delay or shrink the wants, not retirement. Children can get an education loan, but nobody lends you money for retirement.

All schemes for your money

Dark part of each bar = years you pay. Light part = years you wait. ● = when you get your money.

Good to know. Open these at any post office or through the India Post (IPPB) app. RBI Floating Rate Bonds are sold through banks. The rate you lock in stays the same until maturity (PPF and Sukanya change every quarter). Most of these are taxable, except PPF and Sukanya Samriddhi. In the old tax regime, PPF, NSC, Sukanya, SCSS and the 5-year Time Deposit also give an 80C deduction (up to ₹1.5 lakh in total).

Please read. This is an educational tool, not personal financial advice, and it was not made by a SEBI-registered advisor. Returns for market-linked options are planning assumptions based on long-term Indian market history. They are not promises: real returns can be much higher or lower, and you can lose money. The example funds are well-known, large funds in each category, listed in no order. They are not recommendations, so check the latest returns and costs before investing. Always choose the Direct – Growth version.
